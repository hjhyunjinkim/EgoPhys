Data_Examples:
Contains examples of our dataset. All videos are captured with the Meta Project Aria Gen 1. 

Generalization_Experiments:
1. Ours_Generalization - Contains videos of EgoPhys tested on unseen objects and interactions. Our method is trained on the same videos with specific objects and interactions and tested on unseen objects and interactions.
2. PhysTwin_Generalization - Contains videos of PhysTwin tested on unseen objects and interactions. Our method is trained on the same videos with specific objects and interactions and tested on unseen objects and interactions

Main_Experiments:
1. Ours_Video - Contains videos of EgoPhys tested on per-object basis. 
2. PhysTwin_Video - Contains videos of PhysTwin tested on per-object basis. 
3. SpringGaus_Video - Contains videos of Spring-Gaus tested on per-object basis. 

Ours_Robot_Experiments:
Contains videos of sim-to-real transfer on a physical XArm6 robot. Given a single egocentric human video of a novel deformable object, EgoPhys builds a digital twin, plans toward a target configuration, and transfers the waypoints to the robot
